Raplo Capture

AI conversation capture for events and venues. An exhibitor or host meets a visitor, the visitor agrees to be captured, and Raplo Capture handles the rest: it transcribes the conversation, works out who this person is and what they wanted, writes the recap, and sends the follow-up.

  • Consented capture of contact details and conversation
  • Transcription and qualification, automatically
  • Instant recap for both sides
  • Follow-up that goes out without anyone remembering to send it
