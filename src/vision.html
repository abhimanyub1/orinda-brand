Vision

Where Orinda Labs is going.

Our thesis: Orinda Labs is building the AI layer for in-person conversations at events and venues — not another lead-capture tool.

Every capture is a rare, high-signal moment. A real person, face to face, expressed intent about something — and consented to be contacted about it. There are not many moments like that, and almost nothing else in software gets to start from one.

Today, tools built on those moments treat the person as a row to extract. The value accrues to one side and evaporates after one event.

Orinda Labs is building the two-sided, durable version. The attendee gets an AI that remembers and acts on everything they cared about, across every event they go to. The host — the exhibitor, the venue, the organizer — gets an AI that captures the room and follows through automatically. Both sides get something from the same moment, which is the only arrangement in which both sides keep showing up.

The result is something only consent, first-party capture, and two-sided adoption can produce together: a consented map of real-world intent — expressed in person, owned and controllable by the people in it. Not inferred from browsing. Not bought from a broker. Given, deliberately, by someone standing in front of you at an event.

Our consent commitment

None of this works without consent, so consent is not a setting we bolt on afterwards. A visitor’s details and conversation enter Raplo Capture only when that visitor has explicitly consented. That holds for every product on this page, shipped or in development.

The person the data is about controls it and can have it deleted. We do not capture people who have not agreed to be captured, we do not buy or sell personal data, and we do not build profiles of people from sources they did not consent to.

How we handle personal information, and how to exercise your rights over it, is set out in our privacy policy. Questions go to privacy@orindalabs.com.
