Where this is going

Not another lead-capture tool.

Every capture is a rare, high-signal moment: a real person, face to face, expressing intent about something — and consenting to be contacted. Tools built on those moments usually treat the person as a row to extract, so the value accrues to one side and evaporates after one event.

Orinda Labs is building the two-sided, durable version: the attendee gets an AI that remembers and acts on what they cared about across every event, and the host gets an AI that captures the room and follows through. What that produces — and only consent plus first-party capture can produce it — is a consented map of real-world intent, owned and controllable by the people in it.
