What we do

Four things, done properly.

We are deliberately narrow. Every engagement lands in one of these, and every one of them ends with running software — not a slide deck.

Document intelligence

Contracts, invoices, claims, statements, scanned nightmares. We build capture pipelines that read them, structure them, and know when they are unsure.

  • OCR + layout-aware extraction
  • Schema-validated output
  • Confidence scoring & human review loops

Retrieval & knowledge

Search that answers instead of guessing. Chunking, embeddings, reranking and citations, tuned against your own corpus rather than a benchmark.

  • Hybrid vector + keyword retrieval
  • Grounded answers with citations
  • Permission-aware indexes

Agentic workflows

Multi-step automations with real guardrails: tool budgets, retries, idempotency, audit trails, and a stop button that actually stops things.

  • Tool-using agents with budgets
  • Deterministic orchestration
  • Full run traces & replay

Evaluation & reliability

The unglamorous part that decides whether any of it survives contact with production. Golden sets, regression gates, drift alerts, cost ceilings.

  • Golden datasets & scorers
  • CI gates on every model change
  • Latency and spend budgets

Our product

Raplo Capture turns any document into clean, structured data.

Drop in a PDF, a photo of a receipt, a forty-page contract or a fax from 1998. Raplo Capture reads it, extracts the fields you asked for, validates them against your schema, and flags anything it is not sure about — so a human only ever looks at the 3% that needs a human.

  • Point-and-click field schemas — no model training required
  • Per-field confidence with a built-in review queue
  • Webhooks and a REST API that fit into what you already run
  • Your data stays in your tenant

How we work

Small team. Short loops. No theatre.

  1. 01

    Scope in a week

    We take your hardest real examples — not the clean ones — and come back with a written scope, a success metric, and a fixed price.

  2. 02

    Prototype in three

    A working thing you can click, measured against a golden set from day one. If the numbers do not move, we say so early.

  3. 03

    Harden for production

    Auth, rate limits, observability, cost caps, CI eval gates, and runbooks. The boring layer that keeps it alive.

  4. 04

    Hand over, properly

    Your repo, your cloud, your keys. Documentation and a walkthrough, plus a support window while your team takes the wheel.
