About us

We are building software that understands the room.

Orinda Labs is an early-stage software company. We think the most valuable thing a person can tell you is still the thing they say out loud, to your face — and that almost no software has ever been able to hear it.

So that is what we build: ambient intelligence. Software that understands real conversations between real people, with their consent, and turns them into something useful — a recap, a follow-up, an introduction that actually gets made.

Our first product, Raplo Capture, does this for events and venues, and it is live today. Events are where we started because the consent is unambiguous and the conversation is worth something to both people in it. They are the first application of the technology, not the last.

What we believe

Consent is the product, not the paperwork. Nothing enters our software unless the person it is about has agreed, and they can have it deleted. That is a harder way to build and it is the only way this is worth building.

Both sides, or neither. Tools built on face-to-face moments usually serve one side and treat the other as a row to extract. We think the version that lasts gives something back to the person who spoke.

Ship it, then talk about it. We would rather hand you working software than a roadmap. The one product we sell is the one you can use this afternoon.

Working with us

We are small and early. There is no formal listing and no recruiting process to grind through — if this is the problem you want to spend a few years on, write to info@orindalabs.com and tell us what you would build.
